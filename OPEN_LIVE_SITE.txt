What is in this folder

- README.md - short project notes.

Google Drive
Google Drive stores and previews files; it does not host websites. Opening index.html from a Drive
link will show a preview or download, not a working page. Use the live link above on any device.

To share this folder from Drive:
1. Upload this whole folder to Google Drive.
2. Right-click the folder and choose Share.
3. Choose who can view it, then copy the link.
4. Include the live website link above so people can open the actual page.

Local / offline
Double-click index.html. It works without internet access and without any other files.
